# Pretérito indefinido: regular verbs

The preterite (*pretérito indefinido*) is the Spanish simple past: *Ayer hablé con Marta.* (Yesterday I talked to Marta.) It tells what happened at a finished moment, like English "talked" or "ate". English needs "did" for questions and negatives; Spanish does not: *¿Hablaste con ella?* (Did you talk to her?) Watch the accents: *hablo* is "I speak", *habló* is "he / she / you spoke".

## How it works

Drop *-ar*, *-er* or *-ir* and add the ending; *-er* and *-ir* share one set.

| Person | hablar | comer | vivir |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| yo | habl**é** | com**í** | viv**í** |
| tú | habl**aste** | com**iste** | viv**iste** |
| él, ella, usted | habl**ó** | com**ió** | viv**ió** |
| nosotros, nosotras | habl**amos** | com**imos** | viv**imos** |
| vosotros, vosotras (Spain) | habl**asteis** | com**isteis** | viv**isteis** |
| ellos, ellas, ustedes | habl**aron** | com**ieron** | viv**ieron** |

- The *yo* and *él / ella / usted* forms have a written accent: *hablé, habló, comí, comió*.
- *Hablamos* and *vivimos* look like the present; the time word tells you which: *Ayer hablamos.*
- The *tú* form has no *-s* at the end: *hablaste*.
- Stem-changing verbs in *-ar* and *-er* do not change in the preterite: *cerrar → cerré*, *volver → volví*, *jugar → jugué*.

**Spelling changes in the yo form:**

| Infinitive | yo | él, ella, usted |
| --- | --- | --- |
| buscar, tocar (-car) | bus**qué**, to**qué** | buscó, tocó |
| llegar, pagar (-gar) | lle**gué**, pa**gué** | llegó, pagó |
| empezar (-zar) | empe**cé** | empezó |

*Leer*: *le**yó***, *le**yeron***.

**Time words:** *ayer* (yesterday), *anteayer* (the day before yesterday), *anoche* (last night), *el lunes pasado* (last Monday), *la semana pasada* (last week), *el año pasado* (last year), *hace dos años* (two years ago).

## When to use it

1. **A finished action.** *Ayer compré pan.* (Yesterday I bought bread.)
2. **Actions one after the other.** *Llegué a casa, cené y me acosté.* (I got home, had dinner and went to bed.)
3. **A closed period.** *Viví tres años en Lima.* (I lived in Lima for three years.)
4. **Questions and negatives, without "did".** *¿Cenaste en casa? —No, no cené.* (Did you have dinner at home? No.)
5. **"Ago": *hace*.** *Terminé la universidad hace cinco años.* (I finished college five years ago.)

## Examples

| Spanish | English |
| --- | --- |
| Anoche cenamos en un restaurante peruano. | Last night we had dinner at a Peruvian restaurant. |
| ¿A qué hora llegaste? | What time did you arrive? |
| Mis padres vivieron dos años en Chile. | My parents lived in Chile for two years. |
| Ayer no bebí café. | I didn't drink coffee yesterday. |
| El concierto empezó a las nueve. | The concert started at nine. |
| Busqué las llaves por toda la casa. | I looked for the keys all over the house. |

## Good to know

- In Spain, for things that happened earlier today (*hoy*, *esta mañana*), people usually use another past tense: see [[a2-verbs-present-perfect]]. In most of Latin America the preterite is normal for today too: *Hoy hablé con Marta.* With *ayer* or *el año pasado*, everyone uses the preterite.

## Common mistakes

- ✗ *Ella hablo con el profesor.* → ✓ *Ella habló con el profesor.* — without the accent, *hablo* means "I speak".
- ✗ *¿Comistes en casa?* → ✓ *¿Comiste en casa?* — the *tú* form of the preterite has no final *-s*.
- ✗ *Ayer como con mis amigos.* → ✓ *Ayer comí con mis amigos.* — a finished past action needs the preterite.
- ✗ *Anoche comé pizza.* → ✓ *Anoche comí pizza.* — *-er* verbs take *-í*, not the *-ar* ending *-é*.
- ✗ *Buscé las llaves.* → ✓ *Busqué las llaves.* — *-car* becomes *-qué* in the *yo* form.

## Practice

Write the preterite.

1. Ayer yo ___ (trabajar) hasta las seis.
2. ¿Tú ___ (comer) en casa anoche?
3. Mi hermano ___ (vivir) dos años en Bogotá.
4. Yo ___ (pagar) la cuenta.
5. La película ___ (empezar) tarde.

Translate into Spanish.

6. Did you buy bread? (to a friend)
7. I finished my work two hours ago.

## Answers

1. trabajé
2. comiste
3. vivió
4. pagué
5. empezó
6. ¿Compraste pan?
7. Terminé mi trabajo hace dos horas. / Hace dos horas terminé mi trabajo.
