# Affirmative imperative: instructions and directions

The imperative (*imperativo*) gives instructions: "turn", "come", "take it". English has one form for everybody. Spanish has a form for **tú** (a friend), for **usted** (a stranger, formal) and for the plural: *Gira a la derecha* (to a friend), *Gire a la derecha* (formal). Pronouns are **attached** to the end: *Llámame* (call me), *Dígame* (tell me, the usual way to answer the phone in Spain). This page covers "do"; "don't" has other forms and comes later.

## How it works

**tú: the él / ella form of the present**

| Infinitive | Present (él) | tú imperative |
| --- | --- | --- |
| hablar | habla | **habla** |
| comer | come | **come** |
| subir | sube | **sube** |
| seguir (e → i) | sigue | **sigue** |
| girar | gira | **gira** |

**Eight irregular tú forms**

| decir | hacer | ir | poner | salir | ser | tener | venir |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **di** | **haz** | **ve** | **pon** | **sal** | **sé** | **ten** | **ven** |

**usted and ustedes: start from the yo form of the present, drop -o, swap the vowel**

| Infinitive | yo | usted | ustedes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| hablar | hablo | **hable** | **hablen** |
| comer | como | **coma** | **coman** |
| subir | subo | **suba** | **suban** |
| tener | tengo | **tenga** | **tengan** |
| seguir | sigo | **siga** | **sigan** |
| hacer | hago | **haga** | **hagan** |

- *-ar* verbs take **-e / -en**; *-er* and *-ir* verbs take **-a / -an**.
- Irregular: *ir* → **vaya**, *ser* → **sea**, *saber* → **sepa**, *dar* → **dé**.
- Spelling: *cruzar* → **cruce**, *buscar* → **busque**, *llegar* → **llegue**.

**vosotros (Spain):** infinitive with *-d* instead of *-r*: *hablad, comed, subid, venid*.

**Pronouns** are attached, and the verb often needs a written accent to keep its stress: *llama* → **llámame**, *diga* → **dígame**, *siéntese* (sit down, formal), *pásame la sal*.

## When to use it

1. **Directions.** *Sigue todo recto y toma la primera a la izquierda.* (Go straight on and take the first left.)
2. **Instructions and recipes.** *Corta las cebollas y ponlas en la sartén.* (Cut the onions and put them in the pan.)
3. **Offers and invitations.** *Pasa, pasa. Siéntate.* (Come in, come in. Sit down.) *Toma, un regalo.* (Here, a present.)
4. **Directions and requests to strangers (usted).** *Perdone, ¿la estación? —Siga por esta calle.* (Excuse me, the station? Keep going along this street.)

Add *por favor* to sound polite. Between friends, a question in the present is often softer: *¿Me pasas la sal?*

## Examples

| Spanish | English |
| --- | --- |
| Ven aquí un momento. | Come here a moment. |
| Haz café, por favor. | Make some coffee, please. |
| Llámame esta noche. | Call me tonight. |
| Pase, señora. | Come in, ma'am. |
| Gire a la derecha en el semáforo. | Turn right at the traffic light. |
| Abran sus libros en la página diez. | Open your books to page ten. |
| Dime la verdad. | Tell me the truth. |
| Cruce la plaza y siga recto. | Cross the square and go straight on. |

## Good to know

- *Ve* (go) and *ven* (come) are easy to mix up: *Ve a la tienda* (go to the shop), *Ven a mi casa* (come to my house).
- *Sé* (be) has an accent, like *sé* (I know): *Sé bueno.* (Be good.)

## Common mistakes

- ✗ *Hace la cama.* (to a friend) → ✓ *Haz la cama.* — the *tú* imperative of *hacer* is *haz*.
- ✗ *Me llama mañana.* (as an instruction) → ✓ *Llámame mañana.* — pronouns are attached to the imperative.
- ✗ *Llamame.* → ✓ *Llámame.* — with a pronoun attached, the stressed syllable needs an accent.
- ✗ *Sigua todo recto.* → ✓ *Siga todo recto.* — from *yo sigo*: *siga*.
- ✗ *Viene aquí.* (to a friend) → ✓ *Ven aquí.* — the *tú* imperative of *venir* is *ven*.

## Practice

Write the *tú* imperative.

1. ___ (venir) a mi casa esta tarde.
2. ___ (girar) a la izquierda.
3. ___ (poner) la mesa, por favor.

Write the *usted* imperative.

4. ___ (tomar) la segunda calle.
5. ___ (seguir) recto.
6. ___ (cruzar) el puente.

Translate into Spanish.

7. Call me tomorrow. (to a friend)
8. Tell me, please. (formal)

## Answers

1. Ven
2. Gira
3. Pon
4. Tome
5. Siga
6. Cruce
7. Llámame mañana. / Mañana llámame.
8. Dígame, por favor.
