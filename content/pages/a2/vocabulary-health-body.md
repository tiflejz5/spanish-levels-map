# Body, health and feelings

With parts of the body, Spanish uses *el / la* where English says "my": *Me lavo **las** manos* (I wash my hands). Feelings and states of health take *estar*: *Estoy cansada. Estoy resfriado.* And careful with English "I have a cold": *tengo frío* means "I'm cold". A cold is *un resfriado*.

## Good to know

- *La mano* is feminine although it ends in *-o*: *la mano derecha* (the right hand).
- *Oreja* is the part you can see; *oído* is inside: *Me duele el oído* (I have an earache).
- *Ser* or *estar*: *Es nervioso* (he is a nervous person) / *Está nervioso* (he is nervous now). For how you feel today, use *estar*.

## Common mistakes

- ✗ *Tengo un frío.* → ✓ *Estoy resfriado.* — "a cold" is *un resfriado*; *tengo frío* means "I'm cold".
- ✗ *Me lavo mis manos.* → ✓ *Me lavo las manos.* — with body parts, use *el / la / los / las*.
- ✗ *el mano* → ✓ *la mano* — *mano* is feminine.
- ✗ *Estoy muy embarazada.* (meaning "embarrassed") → ✓ *Me da mucha vergüenza.* — *embarazada* means "pregnant"; "it embarrasses me" is *me da vergüenza*.
- ✗ *Soy cansado.* → ✓ *Estoy cansado.* — feeling tired is a state: *estar*.
