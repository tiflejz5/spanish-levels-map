# Numbers 0–100, days of the week, months

Numbers, days and months are everywhere: prices, phone numbers, plans. Two things surprise English speakers. Days and months are written with a **small letter** in Spanish. And you say "on Monday" without any word for "on": just *el lunes*.

## Words

**Numbers 0–20**

| Spanish | English |
| --- | --- |
| cero, uno, dos, tres, cuatro, cinco | 0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 |
| seis, siete, ocho, nueve, diez | 6, 7, 8, 9, 10 |
| once, doce, trece, catorce, quince | 11, 12, 13, 14, 15 |
| dieciséis, diecisiete, dieciocho, diecinueve, veinte | 16, 17, 18, 19, 20 |

**Numbers 21–100**

| Spanish | English |
| --- | --- |
| veintiuno, veintidós, veintitrés | 21, 22, 23 |
| veinticuatro … veintinueve | 24 … 29 |
| treinta, treinta y uno, treinta y dos | 30, 31, 32 |
| cuarenta, cincuenta, sesenta | 40, 50, 60 |
| setenta, ochenta, noventa | 70, 80, 90 |
| noventa y nueve | 99 |
| cien | 100 |

- 16–29 are **one word**: *diecisiete*, *veintiocho*. From 31 they are **three words** with *y*: *cuarenta y cinco*.
- Watch the accents: *dieciséis*, *veintidós*, *veintitrés*, *veintiséis*.
- *Uno* becomes *un* before a masculine noun and *una* before a feminine one: *un euro*, *una persona*, *veintiún años*.

**Days of the week** (the week starts on Monday)

| Spanish | English |
| --- | --- |
| el lunes | Monday |
| el martes | Tuesday |
| el miércoles | Wednesday |
| el jueves | Thursday |
| el viernes | Friday |
| el sábado | Saturday |
| el domingo | Sunday |
| el fin de semana | the weekend |

**Months**

| Spanish | English |
| --- | --- |
| enero, febrero, marzo | January, February, March |
| abril, mayo, junio | April, May, June |
| julio, agosto, septiembre | July, August, September |
| octubre, noviembre, diciembre | October, November, December |

## Word partners

| Spanish | English |
| --- | --- |
| Hoy es martes. | Today is Tuesday. |
| el lunes | on Monday (this coming Monday) |
| los lunes | on Mondays (every Monday) |
| de lunes a viernes | from Monday to Friday |
| el fin de semana | on the weekend |
| en julio | in July |
| en verano | in summer |
| el número de teléfono | the phone number |
| Mi número es seis, cinco, cero… | My number is six, five, zero… |
| ¿Cuánto es? —Son veinte euros. | How much is it? —It's twenty euros. |

## Good to know

- Phone numbers are read digit by digit or in pairs: 61 20 34 → *sesenta y uno, veinte, treinta y cuatro*.
- *Septiembre* is also spelled *setiembre* in some countries.
- Days ending in *-s* do not change in the plural: *el lunes, los lunes*. *Sábado* and *domingo* add *-s*: *los sábados*.

## Common mistakes

- ✗ *Hoy es Lunes.* → ✓ *Hoy es lunes.* — days and months take a small letter.
- ✗ *en el lunes* → ✓ *el lunes* — Spanish has no word for "on" with days.
- ✗ *veinte y dos* → ✓ *veintidós* — today 21–29 are written as one word.
- ✗ *en Julio* → ✓ *en julio* — months also take a small letter.

## Practice

Write the number in words.

1. 15
2. 22
3. 48
4. 100

Translate into Spanish.

5. on Saturday
6. on Fridays
7. in October
8. Today is Wednesday.

## Answers

1. quince
2. veintidós
3. cuarenta y ocho
4. cien
5. el sábado
6. los viernes
7. en octubre
8. Hoy es miércoles.
