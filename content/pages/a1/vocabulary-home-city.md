# Home, furniture and places in town

With *hay* and *estar* you can now describe where you live: *Mi casa tiene dos dormitorios. Hay un supermercado en la esquina. La farmacia está al lado del banco.* Learn every noun with its article. Words for "apartment" differ by country, so both are given; you will be understood with either.

## Words

| --- | --- |
| la casa | the house, the home |

| el dormitorio | the bedroom |
| el salón (Spain) / la sala (Latin America) | the living room |
| la cocina | the kitchen |
| el baño | the bathroom |
| el jardín | the garden, the yard |

## Word partners

| Spanish | English |
| --- | --- |
| Vivo en un piso pequeño. (Spain) | I live in a small apartment. |
| La cocina es muy grande. | The kitchen is very big. |
| En mi habitación hay una cama y una mesa. | In my room there's a bed and a table. |
| ¿Hay un banco por aquí? | Is there a bank around here? |
| La farmacia está al lado del banco. | The pharmacy is next to the bank. |
| El museo está enfrente del parque. | The museum is opposite the park. |
| La estación está lejos del centro. | The station is far from downtown. |
| La parada de autobús está en la esquina. | The bus stop is on the corner. |
| Mi casa está cerca de la playa. | My house is near the beach. |

## Good to know

- *De* + *el* always becomes *del*: *al lado del banco*, *cerca del parque* (but *cerca de la plaza*).
- *Librería* is a bookstore; a library is *la biblioteca*.
- *El baño* is the bathroom at home; in a restaurant people often ask *¿Dónde está el baño?* or, in Spain, *¿Dónde están los servicios?*

## Common mistakes

- ✗ *Hay la farmacia en la plaza.* → ✓ *La farmacia está en la plaza.* — a known place (*la*) takes *está*.
- ✗ *la librería* (meaning "the library") → ✓ *la biblioteca* — *librería* means "bookstore".
- ✗ *al lado de el banco* → ✓ *al lado del banco* — *de* + *el* becomes *del*.
- ✗ *El hospital está cerca la estación.* → ✓ *El hospital está cerca de la estación.* — *cerca* needs *de* before a place.

## Practice

Write the word with *el* or *la*.

1. kitchen
2. bed
3. pharmacy
4. library

Translate into Spanish.

5. Is there a supermarket near here?
6. The bank is next to the park.
7. My apartment has two bedrooms. (Spain)
8. The station is far from downtown.

## Answers

1. la cocina
2. la cama
3. la farmacia
4. la biblioteca
5. ¿Hay un supermercado cerca? / ¿Hay un supermercado cerca de aquí? / ¿Hay un supermercado por aquí?
6. El banco está al lado del parque.
7. Mi piso tiene dos dormitorios. / Mi piso tiene dos habitaciones.
8. La estación está lejos del centro.
