# tener: possession, age and common expressions

*Tener* means "to have": *Tengo un perro.* (I have a dog.) Spanish uses it in more places than English does. Your age is something you "have": *Tengo 30 años.* And many feelings of the body are things you "have" too: *Tengo hambre* (I'm hungry, word for word "I have hunger"). Where English says "I am…", Spanish often says *tengo*.

## How it works

| Person | tener | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| yo | **tengo** | I have |
| tú | **tienes** | you have |
| él, ella, usted | **tiene** | he / she has, you have (formal) |
| nosotros, nosotras | **tenemos** | we have |
| vosotros, vosotras (Spain) | **tenéis** | you have (plural) |
| ellos, ellas, ustedes | **tienen** | they have, you have (plural) |

- The *yo* form ends in *-go*: *tengo*. The stem changes to *tie-* in *tienes*, *tiene* and *tienen*, but not in *tengo*, *tenemos* and *tenéis*.
- **Negative:** *No tengo hermanos.* After *no*, a single countable thing often has no article: *No tengo coche.* (more natural than *No tengo un coche*)
- **Question:** *¿Tienes hermanos?* (Do you have any brothers or sisters?)

**Expressions with tener** (the noun after *tener* does not change):

| Spanish | English |
| --- | --- |
| tener hambre | to be hungry |
| tener sed | to be thirsty |
| tener calor / frío | to be hot / cold (a person) |
| tener sueño | to be sleepy |
| tener miedo | to be afraid |
| tener prisa | to be in a hurry |
| tener razón | to be right |

To say "very", use *mucho* or *mucha* with the gender of the noun: *mucho frío*, *mucho calor*, but *mucha hambre*, *mucha sed*, *mucha prisa*.

## When to use it

1. **Possession.** *Tengo un coche nuevo.* (I have a new car.)
2. **Family and friends.** *Tenemos dos hijos.* (We have two children.) *¿Tienes novio?* (Do you have a boyfriend?)
3. **Age.** *¿Cuántos años tienes?* (How old are you?) *Tengo veintiún años.* (I'm twenty-one.)
4. **How your body feels.** *Tengo frío.* (I'm cold.) *¿Tienes hambre?* (Are you hungry?)
5. **Agreeing with someone.** *Tienes razón.* (You're right.)

## Examples

| Spanish | English |
| --- | --- |
| Tengo dos hermanos y una hermana. | I have two brothers and a sister. |
| ¿Cuántos años tiene usted? | How old are you? (formal) |
| Mi abuela tiene ochenta años. | My grandmother is eighty. |
| No tenemos coche. | We don't have a car. |
| ¿Tenéis hambre? (Spain) | Are you (all) hungry? |
| Los niños tienen sueño. | The children are sleepy. |
| Tengo mucho calor. | I'm very hot. |
| Tienes razón. | You're right. |

## Good to know

- *Tengo calor* is about how **you** feel. For the weather, Spanish says *Hace calor* (it's hot); you will learn it later.
- *Estar caliente* about a person can have a sexual meaning. Say *tengo calor*.
- *Tener que* + infinitive means "to have to": *Tengo que trabajar.* It comes in A2.

## Common mistakes

- ✗ *Soy 25 años.* → ✓ *Tengo 25 años.* — age takes *tener*, not *ser*.
- ✗ *Estoy caliente.* (I feel hot) → ✓ *Tengo calor.* — the body feeling is *tener calor*.
- ✗ *Tengo mucho hambre.* → ✓ *Tengo mucha hambre.* — *hambre* is feminine (*el hambre*, like *el agua*).
- ✗ *Estoy hambre.* → ✓ *Tengo hambre.* — *hambre* is a noun (hunger), so you "have" it.

Complete with the right form of *tener*.

1. Yo ___ un gato.
2. ¿Cuántos años ___ (tú)?

## Answers

1. tengo
2. tienes
3. tenemos
4. tienen
5. Tengo hambre.
6. Tiene veinte años. / Ella tiene veinte años.
7. ¿Tienes frío?
8. Usted tiene razón. / Tiene razón.
