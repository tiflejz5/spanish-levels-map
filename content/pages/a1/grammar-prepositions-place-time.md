# Basic prepositions of place and time

A few short words do most of the work: *en*, *a*, *de*, *con*, and they do not match English one to one. *En* covers "in", "on" and "at" for places (*en casa*, *en la mesa*), but "at" a clock time is *a* (*a las tres*). And with days Spanish uses no preposition at all: *el lunes* means "on Monday". Learn them in phrases, not as single words.

## How it works

**Place**

| Spanish | English | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **en** | in, on, at (a place) | *en casa*, *en la mesa*, *en el trabajo* |
| **a** | to (movement) | *Voy a Madrid. Voy al banco.* |
| **de** | from, of | *Soy de Perú. el libro de Ana* |
| **desde … hasta** | from … to | *desde Lima hasta Cusco* |
| **entre** | between | *entre el banco y la farmacia* |
| **con** / **sin** | with / without | *café con leche*, *sin azúcar* |
| **para** | for, to (a purpose, a person) | *un regalo para ti* (a present for you) |

Location phrases with *de*: *cerca de*, *lejos de*, *al lado de*, *enfrente de* (opposite), *delante de* (in front of), *detrás de* (behind), *encima de* (on top of), *debajo de* (under). Remember *de* + *el* = *del*.

**Time**

| Spanish | English |
| --- | --- |
| **a** las tres | at three |
| **por** la mañana / tarde / noche (Latin America also: **en** la mañana) | in the morning / afternoon / at night |
| **el** lunes / **los** lunes | on Monday / on Mondays |
| **en** julio, **en** verano, **en** 2026 | in July, in summer, in 2026 |
| **de** nueve **a** cinco | from nine to five |
| **desde** las ocho / **hasta** las ocho | from (since) eight / until eight |
| **antes de** / **después de** comer | before / after eating |

- After a preposition (but not *entre*: *entre tú y yo*), the pronouns *yo* and *tú* become **mí** and **ti**: *para mí*, *para ti*. With *con*: **conmigo**, **contigo**.

## When to use it

1. **Where something is:** *en*. *Las llaves están en la mesa.* (The keys are on the table.)
2. **Where you go:** *a*. *Vamos a la playa.* (We're going to the beach.)
3. **Where something comes from:** *de*. *El tren de Sevilla llega a las diez.*
4. **When:** *a las*, *por la*, *el*, *en*. *Trabajo por la mañana. En agosto no trabajo.*
5. **Who with or for:** *con*, *para*. *¿Vienes conmigo? Este regalo es para ti.*

## Examples

| Spanish | English |
| --- | --- |
| Estoy en el trabajo. | I'm at work. |
| Voy a la oficina en autobús. | I go to the office by bus. |
| La clase es de diez a doce. | The class is from ten to twelve. |
| Nos vemos el sábado por la tarde. | See you on Saturday afternoon. |
| En invierno hace frío. | It's cold in winter. |
| El gato está debajo de la cama. | The cat is under the bed. |
| ¿Quieres venir conmigo? | Do you want to come with me? |
| Me lavo los dientes después de comer. | I brush my teeth after eating. |

## Good to know

- Means of transport take *en*: *en coche, en tren, en avión*. But "on foot" is *a pie* or *caminando*.

## Common mistakes

- ✗ *en el lunes* → ✓ *el lunes* — no preposition with days.
- ✗ *en las tres* → ✓ *a las tres* — "at" a clock time is *a*.
- ✗ *Voy en la playa.* → ✓ *Voy a la playa.* — movement to a place takes *a*.
- ✗ *con yo* → ✓ *conmigo* — "with me" is one word.
- ✗ *para tú* → ✓ *para ti* — after a preposition *tú* becomes *ti*.

## Practice

Write the preposition or the article.

1. Vivo ___ Madrid.
2. Voy ___ la universidad.
3. La tienda abre ___ las nueve.
4. Trabajo ___ la mañana.
5. Mi cumpleaños es ___ mayo.
6. Nos vemos ___ viernes.

Translate into Spanish.

7. with me
8. a coffee with milk and no sugar

## Answers

1. en
2. a
3. a
4. por
5. en
6. el (no preposition, but the article: nos vemos el viernes)
7. conmigo
8. un café con leche sin azúcar / un café con leche y sin azúcar
