# Present tense of regular -ar, -er, -ir verbs

Every Spanish verb ends in **-ar**, **-er** or **-ir** in the dictionary: *hablar* (to speak), *comer* (to eat), *vivir* (to live). In the present tense you take off that ending and add a new one that shows **who** does the action. English adds only *-s* (I speak, she speaks); Spanish has six endings, one per person. The good news: once you know the pattern, it works for hundreds of regular verbs.

## How it works

| Person | hablar (to speak) | comer (to eat) | vivir (to live) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| yo | habl**o** | com**o** | viv**o** |
| tú | habl**as** | com**es** | viv**es** |
| él, ella, usted | habl**a** | com**e** | viv**e** |
| nosotros, nosotras | habl**amos** | com**emos** | viv**imos** |
| vosotros, vosotras (Spain) | habl**áis** | com**éis** | viv**ís** |
| ellos, ellas, ustedes | habl**an** | com**en** | viv**en** |

- The **yo** form always ends in **-o**.
- **-er** and **-ir** verbs share every ending except *nosotros* and *vosotros*: *comemos / vivimos*, *coméis / vivís*.
- The pronoun is usually left out, because the ending shows the person: *Hablo inglés.* (I speak English.)
- **Negative:** *no* before the verb: *No como carne.* (I don't eat meat.)
- **Question:** the same verb with ¿ ?: *¿Hablas español?* (Do you speak Spanish?) Spanish has no "do / does".

**Common regular verbs:** *-ar*: *hablar, trabajar* (to work), *estudiar* (to study), *escuchar* (to listen), *llegar* (to arrive), *comprar* (to buy), *tomar* (to take, to drink). *-er*: *comer, beber* (to drink), *leer* (to read), *aprender* (to learn), *comprender* (to understand). *-ir*: *vivir, escribir* (to write), *abrir* (to open), *recibir* (to receive).

## When to use it

1. **Things that are true now.** *Vivo en Bogotá.* (I live in Bogotá.)
2. **Habits and routines.** *Trabajo de lunes a viernes.* (I work from Monday to Friday.)
3. **Actions happening right now.** *¿Qué lees?* (What are you reading?) English uses "-ing" here; Spanish can use the simple present.
4. **Near plans with a time word.** *Mañana trabajo hasta las seis.* (Tomorrow I'm working until six.)

## Examples

| Spanish | English |
| --- | --- |
| Hablo inglés y un poco de español. | I speak English and a little Spanish. |
| ¿Dónde vives? | Where do you live? |
| Mi hermano trabaja en un banco. | My brother works in a bank. |
| No comemos carne. | We don't eat meat. |
| Los niños aprenden muy rápido. | Children learn very fast. |
| ¿Escribís en español? (Spain) | Do you (all) write in Spanish? |
| Mis padres viven en el centro. | My parents live downtown. |
| ¿Lees el periódico todos los días? | Do you read the newspaper every day? |

## Good to know

- Many verbs change their stem or have an irregular *yo* form (*quiero*, *tengo*, *hago*). They come in unit 4; see [[a1-verbs-present-irregular]].
- *Tomar* is used for drinks and food in many places: *Tomo un café.* (I'm having a coffee.)
- The *vosotros* forms carry a written accent: *habláis*, *coméis*, *vivís*.

## Common mistakes

- ✗ *Yo hablar español.* → ✓ *Hablo español.* — the verb needs a personal ending; *hablar* is only the dictionary form.
- ✗ *Él comes pescado.* → ✓ *Él come pescado.* — *-es* is the *tú* ending; *él* takes *come*.
- ✗ *Nosotros vivemos en Lima.* → ✓ *Nosotros vivimos en Lima.* — *-ir* verbs end in *-imos*.
- ✗ *Él come no carne.* → ✓ *Él no come carne.* — *no* goes before the verb.

## Practice

Complete with the right form.

1. Yo ___ (trabajar) en una tienda.
2. ¿Tú ___ (comer) pescado?
3. Ana ___ (vivir) en México.
4. Nosotros ___ (aprender) español.
5. Ellos no ___ (beber) café.

Translate into Spanish.

6. Do you speak English? (to a friend)

## Answers

1. trabajo
2. comes
3. vive
4. aprendemos
5. beben
6. ¿Hablas inglés?

8. No leo el periódico.
