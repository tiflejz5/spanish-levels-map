# Possessives and demonstratives

You have already used *mi* and *mis* in sentences like *Mi hermana es alta.* This page shows the whole set: *mi, tu, su, nuestro, vuestro*. They agree with the **thing owned**, not with the owner: *mis libros* (my books), *nuestra casa* (our house). It also shows the words for "this" and "that". Spanish has three distances instead of two: *este* (this, near me), *ese* (that, near you) and *aquel* (that, far from both of us).

## How it works

**Possessives** (before the noun):

| Owner | one thing | several things |
| --- | --- | --- |
| yo | **mi** casa | **mis** libros |
| tú | **tu** casa | **tus** libros |
| él, ella, usted | **su** casa | **sus** libros |
| nosotros, nosotras | **nuestro** coche / **nuestra** casa | **nuestros** amigos / **nuestras** amigas |
| vosotros, vosotras (Spain) | **vuestro** coche / **vuestra** casa | **vuestros** amigos / **vuestras** amigas |
| ellos, ellas, ustedes | **su** casa | **sus** libros |

- *Mi, tu, su* change only for the plural (*mis, tus, sus*). *Nuestro* and *vuestro* also change for the feminine.
- *Tu* (your) has no accent; *tú* (you) has one.
- *Su* can mean his, her, its, your (formal) or their. If it is unclear, use *de* + the person: *la casa de Ana*, *el coche de ustedes*.

**Demonstratives** (they agree with the noun):

| | near me | near you | far from both |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| masculine | **este** libro | **ese** libro | **aquel** libro |
| feminine | **esta** mesa | **esa** mesa | **aquella** mesa |
| masculine plural | **estos** libros | **esos** libros | **aquellos** libros |
| feminine plural | **estas** mesas | **esas** mesas | **aquellas** mesas |

- Without a noun they mean "this one", "that one": *Quiero este.* (I want this one.)
- *Esto*, *eso* (and *aquello*, far away) refer to something unknown or an idea: *¿Qué es esto?* (What is this?) *Eso es verdad.* (That's true.)
- *Aquí* (here), *ahí* (there, near you) and *allí* (over there) match the three distances.

## When to use it

1. **Talking about your things and people.** *Mi casa es pequeña. Mis padres viven en Lima.*
2. **Asking about someone else's.** *¿Cómo se llama tu perro? ¿Dónde está su hotel, señora?*
3. **Pointing in a shop or a café.** *¿Cuánto cuesta esta camisa? Quiero ese pastel.*
4. **Something far away.** *¿Ves aquella casa? Es de mis abuelos.* (Do you see that house? It's my grandparents'.)
5. **An unknown thing or an idea.** *¿Qué es eso?* (What's that?)

## Examples

| Spanish | English |
| --- | --- |
| Mi madre es profesora y mis hermanos son estudiantes. | My mother is a teacher and my brothers and sisters are students. |
| ¿Es tu coche? | Is it your car? |
| Nuestra casa está cerca del centro. | Our house is near downtown. |
| Señor López, ¿es su maleta? | Mr. López, is it your suitcase? |
| Este café está muy bueno. | This coffee is very good. |
| ¿Te gustan esas fotos? | Do you like those photos (near you)? |
| Aquellos edificios son nuevos. | Those buildings (over there) are new. |
| ¿Qué es esto? | What's this? |

## Good to know

- With body parts and clothes, Spanish often uses *el / la* instead of *mi*: *Me lavo las manos.*
- Texts written before 2010 often have an accent on *éste*, *ése*; today they are written without it.
- In Latin America *aquel* is used less in speech; *ese* often covers both distances.

## Common mistakes

- ✗ *mi libros* → ✓ *mis libros* — the possessive agrees with the things, which are plural.
- ✗ *nuestro casa* → ✓ *nuestra casa* — *nuestro* agrees with the feminine *casa*.
- ✗ *Esto libro es bueno.* → ✓ *Este libro es bueno.* — before a noun, use *este*, not *esto*.
- ✗ *la mi casa* → ✓ *mi casa* — no article before *mi, tu, su*.

## Practice

Write the possessive.

1. (yo) ___ amigos
2. (nosotros) ___ casa
3. (tú) ___ hermana
4. (ellos) ___ padres

Write the right form of *este* or *ese*.

5. ___ (this) mesa
6. ___ (these) libros
7. ___ (that, near you) chaqueta
8. Translate: What's that?

## Answers

1. mis amigos
2. nuestra casa
3. tu hermana
4. sus padres
5. esta mesa
6. estos libros
7. esa chaqueta
8. ¿Qué es eso?
