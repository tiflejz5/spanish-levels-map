# Common irregular and stem-changing verbs in the present

The most useful Spanish verbs are irregular, as in English (*be*, *have*, *go*), but they follow a few patterns. Some have an odd **yo** form (*hago*, *salgo*). Others change a vowel in the **stem**: *querer* → *quiero*, *poder* → *puedo*, *pedir* → *pido*. And a few, like *ir* (to go), you simply learn by heart.

## How it works

**1. Stem changes** (in every form except *nosotros* and *vosotros*):

| e → ie: querer (to want) | o → ue: poder (can) | e → i: pedir (to ask for) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| qu**ie**ro | p**ue**do | p**i**do |
| qu**ie**res | p**ue**des | p**i**des |
| qu**ie**re | p**ue**de | p**i**de |
| queremos | podemos | pedimos |
| queréis | podéis | pedís |
| qu**ie**ren | p**ue**den | p**i**den |

More: e → ie: *empezar* (to start), *pensar* (to think), *preferir* (to prefer), *cerrar* (to close). o → ue: *dormir* (to sleep), *volver* (to come back), *costar* (to cost), *encontrar* (to find). e → i: *repetir* (to repeat), *servir* (to serve). **u → ue:** only *jugar* (to play): *juego, juegas, juega, jugamos, jugáis, juegan*.

**2. Irregular yo form, the rest regular:**

| Verb | yo | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| hacer (to do, to make) | **hago** | *Hago la cena.* |
| salir (to go out, to leave) | **salgo** | *Salgo a las ocho.* |
| poner (to put) | **pongo** | *Pongo la mesa.* |
| traer (to bring) | **traigo** | *Traigo vino.* |
| conocer (to know a person or place) | **conozco** | *Conozco Madrid.* |
| saber (to know a fact) | **sé** | *No sé.* |
| ver (to see) | **veo** | *Veo la tele.* |

**3. Fully irregular: ir (to go):** *voy, vas, va, vamos, vais, van*. Usually with *a* (remember *a* + *el* = *al*): *Voy a la playa. Vamos al cine.*

## When to use it

1. **Saying what you want and can do.** *Quiero un café. ¿Puedes venir mañana?* (Can you come tomorrow?)
2. **Talking about your week.** *Los sábados juego al fútbol y salgo con amigos.*
3. **Saying where you go.** *Voy al trabajo en autobús.* (I go to work by bus.)
4. **Asking for things.** *Pido una pizza.* (I order a pizza.)

## Examples

| Spanish | English |
| --- | --- |
| ¿Quieres un té? | Would you like a cup of tea? |
| No puedo, trabajo mañana. | I can't, I'm working tomorrow. |
| ¿A qué hora empieza la clase? | What time does the class start? |
| Los domingos duermo mucho. | On Sundays I sleep a lot. |
| ¿Cuánto cuesta? | How much is it? |
| Mis hijos juegan al tenis. | My kids play tennis. |
| Hago deporte dos veces a la semana. | I exercise twice a week. |
| Vamos a la playa en agosto. | We go to the beach in August. |

## Good to know

- *Jugar a* + game: *jugar al fútbol*, *jugar a las cartas*. In parts of Latin America people also say *jugar fútbol*.
- *Saber* is for facts and skills (*Sé nadar*, I can swim); *conocer* is for people and places (*Conozco a Ana*). Before a person, *conocer* takes *a*.
- *Pedir* is "to ask for" something; *preguntar* is "to ask" a question.

## Common mistakes

- ✗ *Yo quero un café.* → ✓ *Yo quiero un café.* — *querer* changes e to ie in the stem.
- ✗ *Nosotros quieremos.* → ✓ *Nosotros queremos.* — no stem change in *nosotros* and *vosotros*.
- ✗ *Yo hace la cena.* → ✓ *Yo hago la cena.* — the *yo* form of *hacer* is *hago*.
- ✗ *Yo sabo.* → ✓ *Yo sé.* — the *yo* form of *saber* is *sé*.
- ✗ *Conozco que es tarde.* → ✓ *Sé que es tarde.* — facts take *saber*; *conocer* is for people and places.

## Practice

Complete with the right form.

1. Yo no ___ (poder) venir hoy.
2. ¿Tú ___ (querer) un café?
3. Nosotros ___ (dormir) ocho horas.
4. Yo ___ (hacer) la cena.
5. Ellos ___ (ir) a la playa.

Translate into Spanish.

6. I'm going out with my friends. (salir)
7. What time does the movie start? (empezar)
8. I don't know.

## Answers

1. puedo
2. quieres
3. dormimos
4. hago
5. van
6. Salgo con mis amigos.
7. ¿A qué hora empieza la película?
8. No sé.
