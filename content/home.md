# Spanish Levels Map

A map of Spanish from A1 to C2, with every explanation in English. For each level it shows what you need to know, explains each point with examples, and lets you practice it. You tick off what you know and see your way to the top.

## How to start

- **Not sure of your level?** [Take the placement test (6 minutes)](#/placement). It tells you where to start on the trail.
- **Starting from zero?** [Start the trail at A1](#/trail). The first stop is the alphabet, pronunciation and stress.
- **Looking for one thing?** Press `/` and search, or open a level in the menu.

## How it works

- **The trail.** Each level is a land with stops, one per topic, and a checkpoint at the end of each unit. Read the page, take the four-question quiz and move on. You can skip what you already know.
- **I know this.** Tick a topic when you are sure of it. Quizzes never tick it for you: the list shows what *you* think you know.
- **Review.** Mistakes from quizzes and sets go to your mistake log and come back later, together with your flashcards. **Today** shows what is due and where you are on the trail.
- **Tense & Mood Lab.** Every tense and mood on a timeline, tricky pairs side by side, and a few questions that tell you which form fits.

## Privacy

No accounts, no tracking. Your progress is stored only in this browser. Use **Profile → Export data** to keep a copy or to move it to another device.

## Found a mistake?

The content is new and checked carefully, but mistakes happen. Every topic page, quiz and set has a **Report a mistake** link at the bottom. Thank you!
