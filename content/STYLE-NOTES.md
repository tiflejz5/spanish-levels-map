# Style notes

Rules for every writer and reviewer of content (spec 10 §1). The owner's corrections go here first; the rest are patterns found by the reviews. Every brief of the solver and the proofreader includes this file.

## From the reviews

1. **Label every regional item.** A word, meaning, pronunciation or habit that is not shared by Spain and Latin America gets "(Spain)" / "(Latin America)" or a short note: *papa* (potato, Latin America), letter names *uve* (Spain) / *ve* (Latin America), *buenos días* until noon in Latin America. Accept both forms in tasks.
2. **Common mistakes are errors, not style.** A sentence that is correct but less natural (e.g. an extra subject pronoun) is never marked ✗; explain it in *When to use it* or *Good to know* instead.
3. **One task, one fix.** Find-the-mistake items have exactly one wrong place and one natural fix; a sentence that can be repaired in two ways is rewritten. Matching items must have one possible pairing (check halves and replies for alternatives).
4. **Grammar not taught yet** may appear only as a fixed phrase the page explains or glosses (*¿Cómo estás?*, *somos = we are*). Prompts gloss any new verb form.
5. **Articles with jobs:** no *un / una* before a job on its own (*Es médico*), but avoid items where *un* could be defended (a job with a complement).
6. **Accepted answers** include with / without the subject pronoun, *usted* with and without *usted*, *Ud.*, and the Latin American variants that the page mentions (*acá*).
7. **English:** American spelling (*traveling*, *color*), short sentences; speaker labels in dialogues are English (*Tom and Elena*).
8. **Multiple choice:** options must differ after normalisation (case and surrounding punctuation are ignored), so never contrast options only by capitals; questions without a ___ gap in a set use `kind: "meaning"`.
